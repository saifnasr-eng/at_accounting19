AT Full Accounting

Enterprise accounting features for Odoo 19 Community

Adds the accounting capabilities that ship with the Enterprise edition of Odoo to a Community installation, on top of the standard Accounting (account) module.

What's included

  • Asset management — depreciation boards, disposal, revaluation
  • Deferred revenue & expense — spread recognition over periods
  • Bank reconciliation — statement matching widget
  • Customer follow-ups — reminder levels and letters
  • Financial reports — Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Cash Flow, Aged Partner Balance, General Ledger, Trial Balance, Tax Report
