Full Accounting for Odoo Community

Financial reports, assets, deferrals and customer follow-ups — for Odoo 19

Community Odoo gives you the ledger. This adds the layer above it: the statements an accountant is asked for, depreciation that posts itself, revenue and cost spread across the periods they belong to, and a way to chase what customers owe. It sits on top of the standard Accounting (account) module and replaces none of it.

Financial reports

All seven print to PDF, and all filter by date range, journal, account and posted-only or all entries.

  • Trial Balance — opening balance, movement for the period and closing balance per account
  • General Ledger — every entry per account with a running balance
  • Balance Sheet — assets, liabilities and equity at a date, with previous-year and current-year earnings reclassified into equity
  • Profit & Loss — income, cost of revenue and expenses, with gross and net profit
  • Cash Flow Statement — operating, investing and financing, derived from the counterpart of every entry that touched cash, and checked against the actual change in cash
  • Aged Partner Balance — receivable or payable, bucketed by days overdue
  • Tax Report — base and tax amounts per sales and purchase tax

Assets

Straight-line and declining-balance depreciation boards, posted to journal entries by hand or by a scheduled action. Disposal by sale or scrap works out the gain or loss for you, and an asset can be revalued with the board rebuilt from the new value.

Deferrals and follow-ups

Deferred revenue and deferred expense use the same board mechanics as assets, so income and cost land in the periods they belong to rather than the period they were invoiced in.

Customer follow-up levels chase overdue balances, with each reminder posted to the partner's chatter so the history stays with the customer.

Not included

Bank statement reconciliation. It is worth saying plainly rather than leaving you to find out: this module does not add a reconciliation widget. Everything listed above it does.
